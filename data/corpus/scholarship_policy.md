# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Scholarship Policy

**Document Code:** NIT/SCH/2024-25
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2024
**Approved By:** Scholarship Committee

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1. The Northbridge Institute of Technology (NIT) is committed to recognizing academic excellence and supporting students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds through its scholarship programs. This policy outlines the eligibility criteria, application procedures, and terms and conditions for all scholarships offered by NIT.

1.2. Scholarships at NIT are funded through the Institute's endowment, government grants, and contributions from alumni and corporate partners. The total annual scholarship budget is determined by the Finance Committee.

## 2. Categories of Scholarships

2.1. **Merit Scholarship:** Awarded to students who demonstrate outstanding academic performance. Merit scholarships cover between 25% and 100% of tuition fees, depending on the student's academic rank within their batch.

2.2. **Need-Based Scholarship:** Awarded to students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds who demonstrate financial need. Need-based scholarships may cover tuition fees, hostel fees, or both. The extent of financial support is determined based on the family's annual income and assets.

2.3. **Sports Scholarship:** Awarded to students who represent NIT or the state/nation in recognized sporting events. Sports scholarships cover up to 50% of tuition fees and may include additional support for training and equipment.

2.4. **Research Scholarship:** Available to postgraduate and doctoral students who are engaged in sponsored research projects. Research scholarships include a monthly stipend and a tuition fee waiver.

2.5. **Alumni-Funded Scholarship:** Special scholarships established by NIT alumni or corporate donors, each with its own specific eligibility criteria and award amount as defined in the donor agreement.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria

3.1. **Merit Scholarship Eligibility:**
   a) The student must have a CGPA of 8.5 or above at the end of the preceding academic year.
   b) The student must have completed all registered courses without any "F", "FA", or "I" grades.
   c) The student must not have been subject to any disciplinary action during the preceding academic year.
   d) Merit scholarships are reviewed annually. Continuation is contingent on the student maintaining a CGPA of 8.0 or above.

3.2. **Need-Based Scholarship Eligibility:**
   a) The annual family income of the applicant must not exceed ₹8,00,000 (Eight Lakh Rupees).
   b) The student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 6.0.
   c) The student must submit a valid income certificate issued by a competent authority, along with supporting financial documents.
   d) Need-based scholarships are reviewed annually. Continued eligibility requires re-submission of income documentation.

3.3. **Sports Scholarship Eligibility:**
   a) The student must have represented NIT, the state, or the nation in a recognized sporting event during the preceding twelve months.
   b) The student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 5.5.
   c) The sports scholarship requires a recommendation from the Director of Physical Education.

## 4. Application Procedure

4.1. Scholarship applications are accepted during the first four (4) weeks of each academic year. Applications must be submitted online through the NIT Student Information System (SIS).

4.2. Applications submitted after the deadline shall not be considered, except in extraordinary circumstances approved by the Chairperson of the Scholarship Committee.

4.3. Required documents include: academic transcripts, income certificates (for need-based scholarships), sports certificates (for sports scholarships), a personal statement, and two letters of recommendation from faculty members.

4.4. Incomplete applications shall be returned to the applicant for completion. The applicant shall have five (5) working days to resubmit the completed application.

## 5. Selection Process

5.1. Applications are reviewed by the Scholarship Committee, which comprises the Dean of Academic Affairs (Chairperson), two faculty representatives, one student representative, and a representative from the Finance Office.

5.2. For merit scholarships, selection is based primarily on CGPA rank within the applicant's batch. For need-based scholarships, selection considers both financial need and academic standing. For sports scholarships, selection considers the level of sporting achievement and academic standing.

5.3. The Scholarship Committee's decisions are announced within four (4) weeks of the application deadline. The list of awardees is published on the NIT website and communicated to individual recipients via their official email.

5.4. Students may hold only one NIT scholarship at a time. If a student qualifies for more than one scholarship, the scholarship with the higher financial value shall be awarded, unless the student expresses a preference for a different scholarship.

## 6. Disbursement

6.1. Scholarship amounts are adjusted directly against the student's fee obligations. Any remaining balance (for scholarships that exceed fee amounts) is disbursed as a monthly stipend.

6.2. Scholarship disbursement begins from the semester in which the award is made. Scholarships are not awarded retroactively for previous semesters.

## 7. Revocation

7.1. A scholarship may be revoked if:
   a) The student's CGPA falls below the minimum requirement for two consecutive semesters.
   b) The student is found guilty of academic misconduct or any disciplinary offence.
   c) The student provides false or misleading information in the scholarship application.
   d) The student voluntarily withdraws from the program.

7.2. A student whose scholarship has been revoked may reapply in the next application cycle, provided they meet the eligibility criteria at that time.

## 8. Grievance Mechanism

8.1. Students who are dissatisfied with a scholarship decision may submit a written grievance to the Dean of Academic Affairs within ten (10) working days of the announcement of results.

8.2. The grievance shall be reviewed by a three-member panel comprising faculty not involved in the original selection process. The panel's decision shall be communicated within fifteen (15) working days.

## 9. Amendments

9.1. This policy is reviewed annually by the Scholarship Committee and may be amended with the approval of the University Senate.
