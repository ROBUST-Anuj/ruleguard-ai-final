# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Fee Regulations

**Document Code:** NIT/FEE/2024-25
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2024
**Approved By:** Finance Committee, Resolution No. 2024/FIN/018

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1. These regulations govern the fee structure, payment procedures, deadlines, and financial policies applicable to all students enrolled at the Northbridge Institute of Technology (NIT).

1.2. The fee schedule is approved by the Finance Committee on an annual basis and is published on the NIT website and the Student Information System (SIS) at least sixty (60) days prior to the commencement of each academic year.

1.3. All amounts stated in these regulations are in Indian Rupees (₹) unless otherwise specified.

## 2. Fee Structure and Payment Deadlines

2.1. All semester tuition fees must be paid on or before September 15 of the academic year for the Autumn Semester, and on or before February 1 for the Spring Semester. Failure to pay by this date will result in automatic deregistration from all courses.

2.2. The fee structure for the academic year 2024-25 is as follows:

| Fee Component         | Amount (₹)  | Payment Frequency |
|-----------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Tuition Fee           | 1,25,000    | Per Semester       |
| Laboratory Fee        | 15,000      | Per Semester       |
| Library Fee           | 5,000       | Per Semester       |
| Student Activity Fee  | 3,000       | Per Semester       |

### Fee Payment Deadlines

| Fee Type             | Normal Deadline  | Late Fee (if reactivated) |
|----------------------|------------------|---------------------------|

2.3. Students whose registration has been cancelled due to non-payment may apply for reinstatement. Reinstatement requires written approval from the Dean of Academic Affairs and payment of a reinstatement fee of ₹2,000 in addition to all outstanding fees and accumulated late fees calculated from the day after the original deadline.

## 3. Late Fee Policy

3.1. Payments received after the normal deadline but within the late payment window shall be subject to a daily late fee as specified in the table above. The late fee is calculated from the day following the normal deadline until the actual date of payment.

3.2. The maximum late fee that can accumulate for any single fee component is capped at ₹2,500. This cap applies regardless of the number of days of delay within the late payment window.

3.3. Late fees are non-refundable and non-waivable, except in cases of documented financial hardship as determined by the Office of Student Financial Services.

## 4. Fee Payment Methods

4.1. Fees may be paid through any of the following methods:

   a) Online payment through the NIT SIS portal (credit card, debit card, net banking, UPI).
   b) Demand draft drawn in favour of "Northbridge Institute of Technology" and submitted to the Finance Office.
   c) National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) or Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) to the Institute's designated bank account.

4.2. Cash payments are not accepted for amounts exceeding ₹10,000. For amounts of ₹10,000 or less, cash payments may be made at the Finance Office during working hours.

4.3. Payment receipts are generated automatically for online payments. For other payment methods, receipts may be collected from the Finance Office within five (5) working days of payment.

## 5. Fee Refund Policy

5.1. Students who withdraw from the Institute or from individual courses may be eligible for a fee refund according to the following schedule:

| Withdrawal Period                          | Refund Percentage |
|--------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| Before commencement of classes             | 100%              |
| Within first two weeks of instruction      | 80%               |
| Within third to fourth week of instruction | 50%               |
| After fourth week of instruction           | No refund         |

5.2. The Student Activity Fee and Technology Fee are non-refundable after enrollment confirmation, regardless of the date of withdrawal.

5.3. Refund processing takes a minimum of thirty (30) working days from the date of approval of the withdrawal application. Refunds are credited to the same bank account or payment method used for the original payment.

5.4. Students who are dismissed or expelled from the Institute are not eligible for any fee refund.

## 6. Fee Instalment Plan

6.1. Students facing documented financial hardship may apply for a fee instalment plan, which allows the total semester fees to be paid in up to three (3) equal monthly instalments.

6.2. The first instalment must be paid by the normal fee deadline. The second and third instalments are due on the first day of the second and third months of the semester, respectively.

6.3. Applications for the instalment plan must be submitted to the Office of Student Financial Services at least ten (10) working days before the normal fee deadline. Approval is contingent on submission of supporting documentation demonstrating financial need.

6.4. A processing fee of ₹500 is charged for the instalment plan. This fee is non-refundable.

6.5. Failure to pay any instalment by its due date shall result in cancellation of the instalment plan and immediate liability for the full outstanding balance, plus applicable late fees.

## 7. Fee Waivers and Concessions

7.1. Fee waivers are available for students who qualify under the NIT Scholarship Policy. The nature and extent of the waiver are determined by the scholarship category.

7.2. Children of NIT employees (permanent faculty and staff) are eligible for a 50% tuition fee concession, subject to verification by the Human Resources Office.

7.3. Siblings enrolled simultaneously at NIT are eligible for a 10% tuition fee concession for the second and each subsequent sibling.

7.4. Fee waivers and concessions are applied automatically based on verified eligibility. Students must ensure that their eligibility documentation is on file with the Finance Office before the fee deadline.

## 9. Amendments

9.1. The fee structure and these regulations are subject to revision by the Finance Committee. Any changes shall be communicated to students at least sixty (60) days before the commencement of the semester to which the revised fees apply.

9.2. Fee increases shall not exceed 10% per academic year unless approved by the Board of Governors in extraordinary circumstances.
