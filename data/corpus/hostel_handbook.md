# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Hostel Handbook

**Document Code:** NIT/HOS/2024-25
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2024
**Approved By:** Chief Warden

---

## 1. Introduction

1.1. The Northbridge Institute of Technology (NIT) provides on-campus residential accommodation for students through its hostel facilities. The NIT campus has six hostel blocks: three for male students (Ashoka, Bhaskara, and Chanakya Hostels) and three for female students (Draupadi, Gargi, and Maitreyi Hostels).

1.2. Hostel residence is available on a first-come, first-served basis. Priority in room allocation is given to first-year students, students from outside the state, and students with documented medical conditions requiring proximity to the campus Health Centre.

1.3. All hostel residents are bound by the rules and regulations set forth in this handbook. Violation of these rules may result in penalties including fines, room reallocation, temporary suspension from the hostel, or permanent expulsion from hostel accommodation.

## 2. Room Allocation and Occupancy

2.1. Room allocation is managed by the Hostel Office under the supervision of the Chief Warden. Students are allocated rooms at the beginning of each academic year. Requests for specific rooms or roommates may be submitted but are not guaranteed.

2.2. Each hostel room is designed for occupancy by two (2) students. Single occupancy rooms are available in limited numbers and require payment of an additional single-room supplement of ₹15,000 per semester.

2.3. Students may not change their allocated room without written approval from the Warden of their hostel block. Unauthorized room changes or swaps shall result in a fine of ₹1,000 and reversion to the originally allocated room.

2.4. At the end of each academic year, students must vacate their rooms within forty-eight (48) hours of the completion of their last examination. Room inventory inspection must be completed before vacating.

## 3. Hostel Fees

3.1. The hostel fee for the academic year 2024-25 is as follows:

| Accommodation Type   | Fee per Semester (₹) |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| Double Occupancy     | 25,000               |
| Single Occupancy     | 40,000               |

3.2. The hostel fee includes accommodation, common area maintenance, Wi-Fi access, and laundry facility access. It does not include mess (dining) charges, which are billed separately.

3.3. Hostel fees must be paid according to the schedule specified in the Fee Regulations. Late payment will attract a late fee of ₹250 per day, as specified in the Fee Regulations.

3.4. Students who vacate the hostel before the end of the semester are not eligible for a pro-rata refund of hostel fees. However, if a student withdraws from the Institute entirely, the fee refund schedule in the Fee Regulations shall apply.

## 4. Hostel Timings and Access

4.1. The hostel gates are open from 6:00 AM to 10:30 PM on weekdays and from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM on weekends and holidays. Students who need to enter or exit the hostel outside these hours must obtain prior permission from the Warden or the night duty officer.

4.2. All visitors must register at the hostel reception desk. Visitors are permitted in the common areas only and are not allowed in student rooms. Visiting hours are from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM on weekdays and from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekends.

4.3. Overnight guests are not permitted in the hostels under any circumstances. Students who wish to have family members visit overnight must arrange external accommodation.

4.4. Students who plan to be absent from the hostel overnight must submit an overnight absence form to the Warden, indicating their destination and emergency contact details.

## 5. Code of Conduct

5.1. The consumption of alcohol, tobacco products, and any illegal substances is strictly prohibited on the hostel premises. Violation shall result in immediate disciplinary action, including potential expulsion from the hostel.

5.2. Gambling in any form is prohibited on hostel premises.

5.3. Students shall not possess or use any of the following items in the hostel: cooking appliances (hot plates, electric cookers, induction heaters), space heaters, weapons of any kind, fireworks, or any item that poses a fire or safety hazard.

5.4. Playing music, musical instruments, or using loudspeakers at volumes that cause disturbance to other residents is prohibited between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM (quiet hours). During quiet hours, noise levels must be kept at a level that does not disturb neighbouring rooms.

5.5. Students are responsible for maintaining cleanliness in their rooms and the common areas. Damage to hostel property (furniture, fixtures, electrical fittings, plumbing) shall be charged to the responsible student(s) at replacement cost plus a penalty of 25%.

5.6. Pets are not permitted in the hostel premises.

## 6. Mess (Dining) Facilities

6.1. Each hostel block has an attached dining hall operated by the Institute's approved catering contractor. All hostel residents are required to subscribe to the mess facility.

6.2. The mess fee for the academic year 2024-25 is ₹4,500 per month. The mess fee is billed monthly and must be paid by the 5th of each month. Non-payment by the 10th of the month shall result in suspension of mess privileges until the outstanding amount is cleared.

6.3. The mess menu is decided by the Mess Committee, which comprises the Warden, two faculty representatives, and four student representatives elected from the hostel residents. The menu is reviewed and updated at the beginning of each semester.

6.4. Students with documented dietary restrictions (medical, religious, or ethical) may request modifications to their meal plan by submitting a written application to the Mess Committee.

## 7. Safety and Security

7.1. All hostel blocks are equipped with CCTV surveillance in common areas, fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, and emergency exits. Students must familiarize themselves with the location of fire safety equipment and emergency exits.

7.2. Fire drills are conducted once per semester. Participation in fire drills is mandatory for all hostel residents.

7.3. Students must report any safety hazard, maintenance issue, or suspicious activity to the Warden or the security office immediately.

7.4. The Institute shall not be held liable for the loss of personal belongings from hostel rooms. Students are advised to secure their valuables and to use the personal lockers provided in each room.

## 8. Disciplinary Provisions

8.1. Minor infractions (noise violations, late return, minor cleanliness issues) shall attract a fine of ₹500 for the first offence and ₹1,000 for subsequent offences.

8.2. Major infractions (substance use, property damage, unauthorized guests in rooms, harassment) shall be referred to the Disciplinary Committee and may result in suspension or permanent expulsion from the hostel.

8.3. Students expelled from the hostel may not reapply for hostel accommodation for a minimum of two (2) semesters.

## 9. Grievance Redressal

9.1. Students who have grievances related to hostel facilities, room allocation, mess quality, or any other hostel-related matter may submit a written complaint to the Warden of their hostel block.

9.2. If the grievance is not resolved satisfactorily by the Warden within ten (10) working days, the student may escalate the matter to the Chief Warden or the Dean of Student Affairs.

## 10. Amendments

10.1. This handbook is reviewed annually by the Chief Warden in consultation with the Wardens of all hostel blocks and the Dean of Student Affairs. Amendments are communicated to all hostel residents at the beginning of each academic year.
