# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Examination Policy

**Document Code:** NIT/EXAM/2024-25
**Effective Date:** August 1, 2024
**Approved By:** Controller of Examinations

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1. This policy governs the conduct, administration, and evaluation of all examinations at the Northbridge Institute of Technology (NIT). It applies to semester-end examinations, mid-semester examinations, supplementary examinations, make-up examinations, and any other formal assessments conducted under the authority of the Controller of Examinations.

1.2. All students, course instructors, examination staff, and invigilators are required to comply with the provisions of this policy. Violations may result in academic or disciplinary action as described herein and in the Disciplinary Regulations.

## 2. Types of Examinations

2.1. **Mid-Semester Examination (MSE):** A written or practical examination conducted during the seventh or eighth week of instruction. The MSE typically carries 20-30% of the total course grade, as specified in the course syllabus.

2.2. **Semester-End Examination (SEE):** A comprehensive written or practical examination conducted at the end of each semester during the designated examination period. The SEE carries 40-50% of the total course grade.

2.3. **Continuous Assessment (CA):** Includes quizzes, assignments, projects, presentations, and laboratory evaluations conducted during the semester. CA typically carries 20-30% of the total course grade.

2.4. **Supplementary Examination:** An examination offered to students who have failed a course, as described in Section 7 of this policy.

2.5. **Make-Up Examination:** An examination offered to students who missed a scheduled examination due to an approved reason, as described in Section 8 of this policy.

## 3. Examination Schedule

3.1. The examination schedule for semester-end examinations is prepared by the Controller of Examinations and published at least three (3) weeks before the commencement of the examination period.

3.2. Mid-semester examinations are scheduled by the respective departments in coordination with the Controller of Examinations. The schedule must be communicated to students at least two (2) weeks in advance.

3.3. No student shall be required to take more than two examinations on a single day. If the published schedule places a student in such a situation, the student may request a rescheduling by submitting a written application to the Controller of Examinations at least one (1) week before the examination date.

3.4. The examination timetable, once published, shall not be altered except in extraordinary circumstances approved by the Vice-Chancellor.

## 4. Eligibility for Examinations

4.1. A student's eligibility to appear for the semester-end examination in any course is contingent upon meeting the minimum attendance requirements as specified in the Attendance Policy (minimum 75% attendance in that course).

4.2. Students must have paid all outstanding fees, including examination fees, before the commencement of the examination period. The examination fee for regular semester examinations is ₹2,000 per semester.

4.3. Students who have been suspended or expelled from the Institute are not eligible to appear for any examination during the period of their suspension or expulsion.

4.4. Students on Academic Probation are permitted to appear for examinations but may be subject to additional monitoring.

## 5. Conduct During Examinations

5.1. Students must carry their NIT Identity Card to every examination session. Students without a valid ID card may be denied entry to the examination hall.

5.2. Electronic devices, including mobile phones, smartwatches, and tablets, are strictly prohibited in the examination hall. Calculators are permitted only where explicitly allowed by the course instructor, and must be of the non-programmable type.

5.3. Students must arrive at the examination hall at least fifteen (15) minutes before the scheduled start time. Students who arrive more than thirty (30) minutes after the start of the examination shall not be permitted to enter the examination hall.

5.4. No student shall be permitted to leave the examination hall during the first sixty (60) minutes or the last fifteen (15) minutes of the examination.

5.5. All answer scripts must be submitted to the invigilator at the end of the examination. Failure to submit an answer script shall result in the student receiving zero marks for that examination.

## 6. Evaluation and Results

6.1. Answer scripts for semester-end examinations shall be evaluated by the course instructor within ten (10) working days of the examination. Results shall be submitted to the Controller of Examinations for processing and publication.

6.2. Results are published through the NIT Student Information System. Grade cards are issued to students within thirty (30) days of the publication of results.

6.3. A student who believes that their examination performance has been unfairly evaluated may apply for revaluation. Revaluation requests must be submitted within ten (10) working days of the publication of results, along with a revaluation fee of ₹500 per course. The revaluation shall be conducted by an independent evaluator who was not the original examiner.

6.4. The outcome of a revaluation is final and binding. The revised grade (whether higher, lower, or unchanged) shall replace the original grade on the student's transcript.

## 7. Supplementary Examinations

7.1. A student who fails a course (receives an "F" grade) shall be permitted to appear for exactly one supplementary examination for that course. No further supplementary attempts shall be allowed.

7.2. Supplementary examinations are scheduled during the Summer Term or at the beginning of the following semester, as determined by the Controller of Examinations.

7.3. The supplementary examination fee is ₹1,000 per course. This fee must be paid at the time of application.

7.4. The maximum grade that can be awarded in a supplementary examination is "D" (6 grade points), regardless of the student's actual score.

7.5. Students who fail a supplementary examination must re-register for the course and complete all requirements afresh, including attendance and continuous assessment.

7.6. Applications for supplementary examinations must be submitted within two (2) weeks of the declaration of results. Late applications will not be accepted under any circumstances.

## 8. Make-Up Examinations

8.1. Make-up examinations are provided for students who miss a scheduled mid-semester or semester-end examination due to an approved reason. Approved reasons include:

   a) Documented medical emergency, supported by a certificate from the NIT Health Centre or a recognized hospital.
   b) Death of an immediate family member, supported by appropriate documentation.
   c) Participation in an NIT-sponsored event that conflicts with the examination schedule, with prior approval from the Head of the Department.

8.2. Applications for make-up examinations must be submitted within five (5) working days of the missed examination, along with supporting documentation.

8.3. Make-up examinations are typically scheduled within two (2) weeks of the original examination date. The format and difficulty level of the make-up examination shall be equivalent to the original examination.

8.4. Unlike supplementary examinations, there is no grade cap for make-up examinations. Students are eligible for the full range of grades.

## 9. Examination Malpractice

9.1. Any act of cheating, dishonesty, or misconduct during an examination constitutes examination malpractice. This includes, but is not limited to: possession of unauthorized materials, communicating with other students, copying from another student's work, impersonation, and attempting to influence examination personnel.

9.2. If an invigilator suspects malpractice, they shall confiscate any unauthorized materials, record the details of the incident, and submit a written report to the Controller of Examinations within twenty-four (24) hours.

9.3. Suspected malpractice cases are referred to the Examination Misconduct Committee, which shall conduct a hearing within ten (10) working days. The student shall be given an opportunity to present their defence.

9.4. Penalties for examination malpractice include: cancellation of the examination for that course; award of an "F" grade; suspension from examinations for one or two subsequent semesters; or expulsion from the Institute in cases of repeated or severe misconduct.

## 10. Special Examination Arrangements

10.1. Students with documented disabilities may apply for special examination arrangements through the Office of Disability Services. Accommodations may include additional time (up to 50% extra), a separate examination room, use of assistive technology, or an amanuensis.

10.2. Requests for special arrangements must be submitted at least two (2) weeks before the commencement of the examination period.

## 11. Amendments

11.1. This policy may be amended by the Controller of Examinations with the approval of the University Senate. All amendments shall be communicated to students at least thirty (30) days before the commencement of the relevant examination period.
